# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful and considerate**: Treat others with dignity and respect. Different experiences and viewpoints strengthen our project.
- **Be inclusive**: Welcome newcomers and help them learn our norms and practices.
- **Be collaborative**: Work together to resolve conflicts, constructively disagree, and find common ground.
- **Be focused**: Keep discussions on topic and productive for the community.
- **Be professional**: Maintain professionalism in all interactions, both public and private.
- **Be empathetic**: Seek to understand others' perspectives before expressing your own.

### Unacceptable Behavior

- **Harassment**: Any unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics. This includes offensive comments, slurs, personal attacks, or trolling.
- **Discrimination**: Excluding or treating someone differently based on their identity or background.
- **Privacy violations**: Publishing private information, such as physical or electronic addresses, without explicit permission.
- **Spam and disruptive behavior**: Posting irrelevant content, repeatedly posting similar messages, or intentionally derailing conversations.
- **Threats or intimidation**: Any language or actions that create a hostile or unsafe environment.
- **Violence**: Encouraging or threatening violence against individuals or groups.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through one of these channels:

### Primary Contact

- **Email**: <conduct@yggdrasil.dev>
- **Private Discord message**: Any project maintainer

### What to Include

1. **Your contact information** (optional, but helpful for follow-up)
2. **Description of the incident**: What happened, when, and where
3. **Individuals involved** (if known)
4. **Any witnesses** (if applicable)
5. **Screenshots or other evidence** (if available)

### Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment**: We'll respond within 48 hours to confirm receipt
2. **Investigation**: We'll investigate the report thoroughly and confidentially
3. **Resolution**: We'll determine appropriate actions based on our community standards
4. **Follow-up**: We'll communicate resolution to involved parties as appropriate

## Enforcement Guidelines

### Consequences

Based on the severity and nature of the violation, consequences may include:

#### **First Offense**

- Private warning from project leadership
- Request for public apology (if applicable)
- Temporary suspension from community spaces (1-7 days)

#### **Repeated Offenses**

- Extended suspension from community spaces (1-4 weeks)
- Removal from specific project roles or privileges
- Required completion of harassment prevention training

#### **Severe Violations**

- Immediate and permanent ban from all community spaces
- Removal of commit access and other project privileges
- Report to law enforcement (if criminal activity is involved)

### Appeals Process

If you believe a consequence was unfairly applied:

1. **Wait 24 hours** to allow for reflection
2. **Submit a written appeal** to <appeals@yggdrasil.dev>
3. **Include**: Original incident details, why you believe the consequence was unfair, and desired resolution
4. **Review**: A different set of project leaders will review the appeal within 5 business days

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Discord/Slack channels
- Email communications
- Meetups and conferences (virtual or in-person)
- Any other official Yggdrasil community spaces

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the Yggdrasil project in public spaces.

## Project Leadership

### Code of Conduct Committee

- **Maintainers**: Active project maintainers
- **Community Managers**: Individuals responsible for community engagement
- **Advisors**: Trusted community members (as needed)

### Responsibilities

- Enforce this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently
- Provide guidance on interpreting and applying these standards
- Regularly review and update this document
- Lead by example in all community interactions
